Global Achievement
Interlocutor  ·  0–5 marks (half marks permitted)
__
5Handles communication in everyday situations despite hesitation. Constructs longer utterances but cannot use complex language except in well-rehearsed utterances.
4Communicates successfully in most familiar everyday situations with occasional hesitation. Produces connected utterances, though range and complexity remain limited.
3Conveys basic meaning in very familiar everyday situations. Utterances tend to be short — words or phrases — with frequent hesitation and pauses.
2Conveys meaning only in the most predictable situations with considerable effort. Utterances are fragmented and heavily reliant on single words or set phrases.
1Has difficulty conveying basic meaning even in very familiar everyday situations. Responses are limited to short phrases or isolated words with frequent hesitation and pauses.
Grammar and Vocabulary
Assessor  ·  0–5 marks (half marks permitted)
__
5Shows a good degree of control of simple grammatical forms. Uses a range of appropriate vocabulary when talking about everyday situations.
4Shows reasonable control of simple grammatical forms with only occasional errors. Uses adequate vocabulary for most everyday topics, with some repetition.
3Shows sufficient control of simple grammatical forms. Uses appropriate vocabulary to talk about everyday situations.
2Shows partial control of simple grammatical forms; errors are frequent. Uses a limited vocabulary, often relying on basic words and fixed expressions.
1Shows only limited control of a few grammatical forms. Uses a vocabulary of isolated words and phrases.
Pronunciation
Assessor  ·  0–5 marks (half marks permitted)
__
5Is mostly intelligible and has some control of phonological features at both utterance and word levels.
4Is generally intelligible; shows emerging control of word stress and basic intonation patterns, with occasional lapses.
3Is mostly intelligible despite limited control of phonological features.
2Is partially intelligible; phonological errors are frequent and sometimes impede understanding.
1Has very limited control of phonological features and is often unintelligible.
Interactive Communication
Assessor  ·  0–5 marks (half marks permitted)
__
5Maintains simple exchanges. Requires very little prompting and support.
4Maintains most simple exchanges with minimal prompting. Responds adequately but occasionally needs support to keep the interaction going.
3Maintains simple exchanges despite some difficulty. Requires prompting and support.
2Manages only very brief exchanges; frequently loses the thread of the interaction and requires regular prompting.
1Has considerable difficulty maintaining simple exchanges. Requires additional prompting and support.
